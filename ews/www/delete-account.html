About Narsud (نرصد)

Narsud (نرصد) is an early warning system mobile application developed by Sustainable Peace Foundation (مؤسسة السلام المستدام) in partnership with Iraqi Firdaws Association (جمعية الفردوس العراقية). The app is used for reporting incidents and monitoring environmental and community disasters.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data through any of the following methods:

1

In-App Request

Open the Narsud app → Go to Settings → Tap "Delete My Account" → Confirm your identity with OTP → Your deletion request will be processed.

2

Submit the Form Below

Fill out the account deletion request form at the bottom of this page with your registered email and phone number. We will verify your identity and process the request.

3

Email Us Directly

Send an email to abdullahalsalih@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email address and phone number.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data will be permanently deleted:

Data Type Action
Personal Information (name, email, phone) Deleted
Profile Settings & Preferences Deleted
Authentication Tokens & API Keys Deleted
Uploaded Media (photos, audio, documents) Deleted
OTP History & Session Data Deleted

What Data May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained for a limited period after account deletion for legitimate operational and legal purposes:

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Incident Reports (anonymized) Up to 12 months Reports may be retained in anonymized form for public safety and early warning analysis. All personally identifiable information is removed.
Server Logs Up to 90 days Standard server access logs are retained for security monitoring and debugging before automatic deletion.

Processing Timeline

  • Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of identity verification.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.
  • Locally stored data on your device (offline reports, preferences, cache) will remain until you uninstall the app.
  • Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. You will need to register a new account if you wish to use the app again.
