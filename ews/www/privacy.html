Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 5, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Narsud (نرصد). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

When you register for an account, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name (first name and last name)
  • Phone Number – used for account registration and OTP (One-Time Password) verification
  • Province/Region – your selected geographical region

2. Location Data

We collect your device's geographic location (latitude and longitude) when you submit an incident or observation report. Location data is essential for accurately pinpointing the area of the reported incident. Location access is requested only when needed and is used solely for report-related purposes.

3. Media and Files

When creating a report, you may provide the following:

  • Photos and Images – captured from your camera or selected from your device gallery
  • Audio Recordings – recorded through the device microphone for audio observations
  • Files and Documents – uploaded from your device storage

4. Device and Usage Data

We may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Network connectivity status (online/offline)
  • App usage statistics and interaction data

5. Locally Stored Data

The App stores certain data locally on your device, including:

  • Authentication tokens and session information
  • Offline reports that have not yet been synced to the server
  • User preferences and app settings

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account Management – To create and manage your user account, verify your identity via OTP, and maintain your profile settings.
  • Incident Reporting – To process and submit your observation and incident reports, including attaching your location, media files, and descriptions to the report.
  • Offline Functionality – To store your reports locally when you are not connected to the internet and automatically sync them when connectivity is restored.
  • Dashboard and Statistics – To provide you with an overview of your report history and aggregated statistics.
  • Weather Analysis – To provide weather information and AI-powered weather analysis relevant to your location using third-party AI services.
  • Communication – To enable you to share incident reports via SMS, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Service Improvement – To monitor and analyze usage trends to improve the App's functionality and user experience.

Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information may be shared only under the following circumstances:

  • With the EWS Platform – Your reports and personal data are transmitted to the Early Warning System (EWS) backend servers for processing and action by the relevant authorities.
  • Third-Party AI Services – Weather analysis features utilize Google Generative AI services. Only location-related data is shared for this purpose, and it is processed in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
  • At Your Direction – When you choose to share an incident report via SMS, email, or WhatsApp, the report details are shared through the respective third-party communication platform.
  • Legal Requirements – We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is transmitted to and stored on secure servers hosted at ews.tigris-s.tech.
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
  • Offline data is stored locally on your device and is synced to the server when an internet connection becomes available.
  • Authentication is handled via secure OAuth 2.0 tokens.

Data Retention

  • Your personal information and report data are retained for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the information provided below.
  • Locally stored data (offline reports, preferences) can be cleared by uninstalling the App from your device.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access – You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction – You may update or correct your personal information through the App's profile settings.
  • Deletion – You may request the deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw Consent – You may revoke permissions (location, microphone, camera) at any time through your device settings. Please note that revoking certain permissions may limit the functionality of the App.

Permissions Used

The App requests the following device permissions:

Permission Purpose
Location To attach geographic coordinates to incident reports
Microphone To record audio observations as part of a report
Camera / Photo Library To capture or select images for incident reports
Internet Access To submit reports and sync data with the server
Storage To save offline reports and temporary files locally

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Generative AI – Used for weather analysis features
  • OpenStreetMap (via Flutter Map) – Used for map display and location visualization

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your data.
