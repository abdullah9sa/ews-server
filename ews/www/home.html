About the App

What is Narsud?

📡

Incident Reporting

Report environmental and community incidents in real-time with photos, audio, and precise location data.

🌍

Early Warning

Stay informed about potential disasters and environmental threats in your area with real-time alerts and weather analysis.

🤝

Community Support

Connect with authorities and community members to coordinate responses to environmental and social challenges.

Features

What You Can Do

01

Submit Reports

Create detailed incident reports with text descriptions, photos, audio recordings, and GPS coordinates.

02

Offline Mode

Submit reports even without an internet connection. Reports are saved locally and automatically synced when you're back online.

03

Weather Analysis

Get AI-powered weather forecasts and analysis relevant to your location to help predict and prepare for potential hazards.

04

Dashboard & Statistics

Track your submitted reports and view aggregated statistics through a personalized dashboard.

05

Share Reports

Share incident reports via SMS, email, or WhatsApp to raise awareness and coordinate responses.
